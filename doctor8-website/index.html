Questions patients ask

What are early signs of kidney disease?

Many people have no symptoms early on. Swelling, foamy urine, tiredness and high blood pressure can be signs. Blood and urine tests are the only sure way to know.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Bring previous reports, scans, discharge summaries and a list of all your medicines.

Which foods should kidney patients limit?

It depends on your stage and results. Many patients limit salt, and some also limit potassium and phosphorus. Follow the plan your doctor gives you.

How often is dialysis needed?

Most patients on haemodialysis attend three sessions a week. Your doctor decides the schedule from your health and test results.
